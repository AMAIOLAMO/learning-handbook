### Part 1: Mental State Preparation
1. Are you **afraid of starting**?
	1. It may be a lack of [[Mental and Physical Blocks#Executive Functions|Executive Functions]]. Which is common for people who have ADHD, Depression, Anxiety(Which I have :P)
	2. Maybe you don't know how to manage your [[Mental and Physical Blocks#Feasibility Bias Control|Feasibility Bias]]

2. Do you feel like you have **done so much**, **but aren't improving**?
	1. It may be that your mind is lying to you, check this out: [[Mental Misconceptions#Illusion of Improvement|Illusion of Improvement]]

3. Are you **bored out**?
	1. Are you afraid of new things? If not, try something new! Check this out: [[Trying New Things]]
	2. Maybe you have to be brave, and re-evaluate whether or not the thing you are learning is really what you want. Is it your choice or other's "best" choice for you?
